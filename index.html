About

Made by Hand,
Bead by Bead

Wicanhpi's Beadwork is Dakhóta handmade — beadwork created slowly and carefully, the way it has always been done. No two pieces are exactly alike, because no two are made by a machine.

Each design starts as loose glass beads and an idea, then comes together stitch by stitch: earrings, medallions, hoops, and small keepsakes meant to be worn, gifted, and kept.
